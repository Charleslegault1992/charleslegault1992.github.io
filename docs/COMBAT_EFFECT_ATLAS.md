# Combat effect atlas

Place the final sheet at:

`src/assets/images/effects/effects.png`

The sheet uses exact `64x64` cells without padding or margin.

| Row | Columns | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0-2, 3-5, 6-8 | Fire field stages 1, 2 and 3 |
| 1 | 0-2, 3-5, 6-8 | Poison field stages 1, 2 and 3 |
| 2 | 0-2, 3-5, 6-8 | Energy field stages 1, 2 and 3 |
| 3 | 0-2, 3-5, 6-8 | Ice field stages 1, 2 and 3 |
| 4 | 0-4 | Miss poof |
| 5 | 0, 1-4 | Fire projectile and impact/DOT animation |
| 6 | 0, 1-4 | Ice projectile and impact/DOT animation |
| 7 | 0, 1-4 | Energy projectile and impact/DOT animation |
| 8 | 0, 1-4 | Poison projectile and impact/DOT animation |
| 9 | 0-4 | Sword attack |
| 10 | 0-4 | Mace attack |
| 11 | 0-4 | Axe attack |
| 12 | 0-4 | Block or armor absorption spark |

Each field stage loops its three frames. Stage 3 is visual only and deals no damage.
